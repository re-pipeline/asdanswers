Communicate Through Spelling

Communicate Through Spelling is an app designed by Dr. Christian Bogner and Alex Zaharakis for spellers who currently use a spelling board or are learning to use one. It gives them a tablet-based tool that can provide a backing voice, while also providing games that train spelling, motor coordination assessments and challenges, and a way to save spelling sessions and history.

The app is featured on the Apple iPad App Store. It is extremely low cost to use: $39 per year, and free to try for 30 days.
