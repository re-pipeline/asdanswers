Autism is Biomedical

Autism is Biomedical is a not-for-profit foundation that acts as a doorway to help parents enter into the biomedical journey.

AIB offers a focus on 3 developments:

  • Gut Balancing and nutraceutical support: a 10 week program to balance the microbiome.
  • Communicate Through Spelling: a tablet app for spellers.
  • Photobiomodulation trial program: a free 2 week trial of a handheld PBM device for home use.
