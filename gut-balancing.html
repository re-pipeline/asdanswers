Gut Balancing

Gut Balancing is a 10 week program to balance the microbiome, a core driver of ASD symptoms. The program uses an analytical model designed by Alex Zaharakis, responsible for many of the testimonials you see here. It is offered with nutraceutical support through Autism is Biomedical.

There is no cost for participation in the program other than obtaining a 16S type test. More information is available by emailing gutbalancing@autismisbiomedical.com. There may be a waitlist for signup and acceptance.
